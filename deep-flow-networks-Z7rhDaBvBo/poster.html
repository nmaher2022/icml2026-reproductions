1What DFNs are & what we reproduce

A Deep Flow Network is the value function of a min-cost flow whose node balances are affine in x — an expressive surrogate that stays easy to optimize over ℤⁿ. Claims: (1) universality for convex-extendible discrete functions; (2) baseline-level accuracy, far faster integer optimization. Audited from the official code + committed artifacts.

f̂θ(x) = minξ feasible c⁾ξ  s.t.  flow balance = A₂ᵢx + bᵢ  —  convex-extendible by construction
2Claim 1 — universality ★ KEY
Constructive part is exact: hand-built networks on the repo’s own kernel represent 1-D and separable convex functions with zero error; an LP certifies concave targets stay ≥15% wrong for any DFN. SGD fits (bars) show the predicted convex-vs-control separation.

4Claim 2 — accuracy & speed
Official artifacts (3 ground truths, 5 seeds) regenerate the paper tables: DFN best MSE, 28–75× faster than MLP, 3–4 orders vs LSET. Fresh retrain (rightmost): DFN 0.0061 MSE / 0.093 s vs MLP 0.0088 / 1.85 s — same ordering, independent compute.
5Theory link — Δ(A) governs solve time
As training drifts A off its near-identity (M-convex) init, log √det(A) grows 11→51 and re-solve time rises 0.12→3.35 s — the theorem’s Δ(A) dependence in the official artifact data.
6Takeaways ★
  • Both claims reproduce — universality exactly; speed/accuracy via artifacts + fresh retrain.
  • Exemplary release: config-hashed artifacts regenerate tables bit-exact (45/45).
  • Scale ceiling was licensing, not compute: free Gurobi forced n=8; LSET’s exp/log MILP cannot run under it at all.
  • Training budget matters: 400 epochs undertrains; 1 000 restores the edge.
In one sentence: expressive-yet-tractable holds — DFN optimizes 20× faster than an equally accurate MLP.
