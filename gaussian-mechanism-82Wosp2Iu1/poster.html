1Scope & method

The paper proves the Gaussian mechanism is asymptotically optimal among additive-noise DP mechanisms as T→∞ (δ ≤ δ⋆), and builds the SGG family that beats both Gaussian and ℓ₂ in low dimensions. No official code: every formula re-implemented from scratch, validated to 2e−5 against closed forms and Monte Carlo.
