Ellipsoidal Time Series Forecasting: Fern

Models local conditional predictive distributions as data-dependent Gaussian ellipsoids via Brenier's theorem, directly parameterizing the optimal-transport Jacobian's SPD spectral factors (eigenvalues + Householder rotation) through a coupling-network encoder.
Paper: arXiv:2505.17370v6 (Qilin Wang) · OpenReview CoAHlJuMdh · Implementation: from-scratch (Algorithm 1) · Compute: CPU-only, toy scale · paper's own synthetic chaotic benchmarks (Lorenz-63/Roessler/Chua, Appendix A.4.1) used for all 5 claims, vs. DLinear
Outcome — mixed: 1 claim VERIFIED analytically (complexity), 2 TOY-VERIFIED (ablations; nonstationary robustness, partial/system-dependent), 1 REFUTED at toy scale (EPT reversed in all 6 tested scenarios), 1 INCONCLUSIVE (geometric-persistence mechanism not reached at toy horizons). 1 real implementation bug found and fixed via self-audit (an ablation confounded with reduced model capacity) — see BUGFIX_LOG.md. The challenge's own claim-extraction was found stale (numbers don't match any table in the actual v6 PDF) — claims here transcribed fresh from the paper text.

4AblationsTOY

No-encoder catastrophic (+60.5% MSE, EPT collapses 43.8→15.6); no-rotation (+7.6%, all 3 seeds) and no-patching (+10.1%) both consistently worse than base — matches the paper's qualitative ordering. Self-audit found and fixed a capacity confound in the no-rotation ablation.

5Geometry persists past pointwise collapseINCONCLUSIVE

Fern's overall MSE advantage over DLinear holds at every horizon tested (1.3-1.5x lower, h=24 to 192). But DLinear never collapses to mean-guessing in this toy horizon range, so the paper's specific mechanism (baselines collapse; Fern's SWD then stays low) isn't observable — Fern's SWD advantage actually shrinks from 2.44x (h=24) to ~1.0x (h=96-192), the opposite trend. Needs horizons closer to the paper's own scale (720, ~6.5 Lyapunov times) to test the claimed mechanism.

ⓘToy-scale setup

Fern (Algorithm 1) and DLinear reimplemented from scratch in PyTorch, CPU-only. Lorenz-63/Roessler/ Chua generated via the paper's own RK4 equations (Appendix A.4.1). Paper's own MSE/W2/SWD/EPT metrics reimplemented from Appendix A.1. 3 seeds (paper: 4), 6,000-9,000 step trajectories (paper: 25,000-36,000), context/horizon 96 (paper: up to 720). No real-world datasets or heavy baselines (TimeMixer/PatchTST/ Koopa/ModernTCN/PFNN) — DLinear only.

⚠ Bug found and fixed during self-audit (full detail in BUGFIX_LOG.md). The no_rotation ablation originally shrank the OT head's Householder-reflection count (R=1 instead of base R=8), reducing the head's parameter count in addition to bypassing the rotation — confounding "no rotation" with "less capacity" (55,776 base params vs. a smaller pre-fix head). Fixed to keep R fixed and only change the forward-pass computation path (verified equal param counts post-fix); rerun made the ablation's effect consistent across all 3 seeds instead of 2 of 3. Separately, EPT's reversal (Claim 3) and Claim 5's unreached collapse mechanism are disclosed as genuine toy-scale findings, not bugs — investigated for a scale-normalization bug (none found; the paper doesn't describe RevIN/ instance-norm for its synthetic benchmarks) before being accepted and reported honestly.
