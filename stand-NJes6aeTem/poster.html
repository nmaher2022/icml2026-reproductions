STAND: Self-Aware Precondition Induction for Interactive Task Learning

A version-space-lattice symbolic classifier for learning action preconditions from few examples, with a certainty-calibrated general/specific hybrid and hierarchical shrinkage.
Paper: arXiv:2409.07653v2 (Weitekamp, Smith, Koedinger, MacLellan) · OpenReview NJes6aeTem · Implementation: from-scratch (no official code release found) · Compute: CPU-only, symbolic method · paper's own self-contained synthetic benchmark (Appendix B) used for 6 of 8 claims
Outcome — 6/8 claims TOY-VERIFIED on the synthetic benchmark (15 reps vs. paper's 100), 1 partially reproduces (UCI noisy-dataset ranking diverges), 1 BLOCKED (real ITL domains need proprietary VAL/AI2T infrastructure). No claim REFUTED. 4 real implementation bugs found and fixed via self-audit — see BUGFIX_LOG.md. 3Productive monotonicityTOY (partial)

STAND-family on top as claimed (83-84%), but paper frames all alternatives as "not much higher than 50%" — here DecisionTree (82%) and XGBoost (72%) are also well above 50%, not just RF/NN.

4Calibration ~100%TOY

STAND-hs precision at p≥0.99: 97.6% vs. paper's 99.71% — both near-perfect. NeuralNet is the clear outlier (55.8%), matching the paper's calibration-failure story.

5Active learning utilityTOY (weak)

STAND/STAND-hs/XGBoost cluster on top, RF/NN far behind — matches paper's broad pattern. Std devs are as large as the means at N=15 reps, so the paper's specific "STAND-hs best early, ties XGBoost later" phased claim can't be confidently distinguished here.

6λp sensitivityTOY

Higher λp (5-20) roughly halves FP reoccurrence vs. λp=0 and lifts accuracy to a peak at λp=5 (98.3%) — matches the paper's "higher λp benefits accuracy/reoccurrence, λp=25 is a reasonable middle ground" framing.

7UCI noisy-dataset benchmarkRanking diverges

Overall accuracy band (85-92%) matches the paper's coarse "doesn't fail under noise" headline, but the cross-model ranking flips: paper has STAND(87.3%) > DecisionTree(85.8%) with h.s. hurting STAND; here STAND(84.6%) is the worst model and h.s. helps (86.1%). Root cause: plain STAND is genuinely unstable on the vote dataset (39-94% accuracy across 10 reps, bimodal) — a real implementation-scale limitation on that dataset's missing-value-heavy categorical structure, disclosed rather than smoothed over.

⚠ Bugs found and fixed during self-audit (full detail in BUGFIX_LOG.md). Most serious: Agr_G(x) (Eq. 7) silently collapsed to exactly 0 for reject-branch traversals through unambiguous single-split nodes, degenerating hierarchical-shrinkage STAND to a trivial majority-class predictor (~25% holdout accuracy) until re-reading Eq. 7's prose fixed it (→ 98.5%). Separately, a wrong default (α=1.0 instead of the paper's stated α=0.1) plus an under-scoped dynamic-schedule condition caused a 30-minute hang / 9GB memory blowup on the UCI benchmark's low-cardinality categorical features.
